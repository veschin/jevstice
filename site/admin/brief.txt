Console requirements
- Use a visually rich, legible schedule as the main overview: one horizontal common time axis, three berth lanes labeled with name and length limit, blocks positioned and sized by vessel occupancy, with a visible midnight boundary. Show the six-vessel manifest separately with all fields above. An unassigned vessel must be discoverable. Make the visual system feel like a working harbor shift board, not a generic landing page. Choose your own layout, color, and typography; do not use a design description in place of implementation.
- Expose active conflicts on the schedule or manifest: initially Kestrel Strait and Ibis overlap on A. Use text or a symbol with a readable label in addition to any color. Intervals are half-open, so touching endpoints are allowed. Handle midnight by ordering all times relative to the shift start.
- Provide manifest filters for berth (including A, B, C, unassigned, and all berths) and case-insensitive vessel-or-cargo text search. Filters combine and show a useful empty state. Give controls clear, accessible names. Keep manifest filtering distinct from the full schedule so an operator can inspect occupancy behind a filter.
- Provide individually selectable rows, visible selection feedback, and a bulk move to a chosen berth. Reject the entire batch if any selected vessel is too long or any proposed assignment overlaps either a remaining assignment or another moved vessel. Explain the reason, including the affected vessel or berth where possible; do not silently ignore a command or partially move the selected vessels. A disabled destination is acceptable if the reason is visible. Existing unrelated fixture conflicts do not invalidate a move. On success, change both schedule and manifest and recalculate conflicts. Store committed assignments in localStorage and restore them on reload.
- Make the manifest and reassignment controls usable on a 390 px phone viewport without page-wide horizontal overflow. A timeline or table may scroll inside a labeled region. Do not rely only on hover, drag, or color to complete the workflow.
