# Jev tool protocol

Use the matching Jev tool description as the authoritative call template. Fill only its fields; include concise, decision-relevant facts and exact source quotes when relevant.

For `jev_consult`, ask one primary decision. Structured activities such as requirement and plan review may judge multiple separately named items in one call.

Read typed answers only by their stated meaning. A negative, unsupported, malformed, missing or below-floor answer is not approval. After refusal, change only the named failing material and leave passing topics unchanged; never resubmit unchanged material.

Resolve a held finding only with a corrective action that addresses that finding. Do not claim completion until business and architecture acceptance pass at the current revision and the completion check approves. A released boundary remains OPEN; release is not approval.
